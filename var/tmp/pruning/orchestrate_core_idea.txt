    prune_java_egress, under the egress profile, is the one layer-pruner service with a network:
    it keeps or drops the hosts an exercise declared, and the orchestrator does not merge its output.


    The orchestrator mounts the host directory read-write at /var/tmp. The layer pruner's
    containers mount only what they need from it: the exercises and the helpers read-only,
    path_sets read-write, and the verify_* containers the merged opt/core/config read-only.

    Each prune container (the layer pruner, on that language's run-phase image) writes one
    <lang>_<exercise>.cfg and its .json record per exercise into path_sets.

    The orchestrate service then holds every artefact to its record, merges each language's
    exercises into BaseLanguage-<lang>.cfg (no [limits]), writes each exercise's limits (and
    whatever the base lacks, which for a union base is nothing) to exercises/<lang>_<exercise>.cfg, and writes BasePhobos.cfg and TailPhobos.cfg into
    opt/core/config. The verify_java and verify_python services then run every exercise under
    that base and its exercise file and write path_sets/verify/. Copying the result into
    core/config is a reviewed step of its own.

No docker cp and nothing over a network: the shared bind mount is the only channel between them.
